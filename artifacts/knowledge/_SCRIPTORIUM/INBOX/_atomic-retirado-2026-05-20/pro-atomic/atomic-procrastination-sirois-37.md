## Procrastination   Fuschia M. Sirois · CHAPTER 7 / 1. Take responsibility, but donʼt self-blame: Acknowledge and accept that you procrastinated and that this was harmful to you. Accept that your delay was unnecessary and not due to external forces. This is the first step on the journey to self-forgiveness. But itʼs also important to not indulge in self-blame.
