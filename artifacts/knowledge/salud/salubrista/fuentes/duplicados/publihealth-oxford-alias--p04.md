# El Codice de las Corrientes Vivas - Parte 04

## Social activism

- [P129] **HECHO** — HIV/AIDS social activism → policy of treatment access for all HIV+ individuals + recognition of rights of marginalized groups

## The future of public health

- [P130] **HECHO** — successful adding "years to life" but chronic diseases (e.g. Alzheimer's) reduce quality; must now add "life to years"
- [P131] **HECHO** — SARS-CoV-2 pandemic severely impacted global economy + societal functioning; long-term changes difficult to predict

## Introduction — framework

- [P132] **DEFINICION** — determinants of health = "range of personal, social, economic, environmental factors determining health status of individuals/populations" (Nutbeam/WHO 1998)
- [P133] **DEFINICION** — four classical categories: biological, behavioural, environmental, social determinants (Remais & Jackson 2016)

## Biological determinants

- [P134] **DEFINICION** — biological determinants = endogenous body features: genetic structures (body/brain development, disease susceptibility) + age (cell function deterioration)

## Behavioural determinants

- [P135] **HECHO** — tobacco smoking: ~6M premature deaths globally/year (WHO 2015)
- [P136] **HECHO** — obesity prevalence worldwide tripled since 1970s; growing risk in many countries

## Environmental determinants

- [P137] **HECHO** — diarrhoeal disease from contaminated water/food = major cause illness/death in children <5, especially LICs
- [P138] **HECHO** — ~4.2M deaths/year from ambient air pollution + ~3.8M from household air pollution (cooking fuels) (WHO 2018)

## Social determinants — introduction

- [P139] **DEFINICION** — SDH = daily social/economic living conditions + higher-level socioeconomic/political factors affecting distribution of power/money/resources (CSDH 2008)
- [P140] **DEFINICION** — key SDH mediating mechanisms: power, income/wealth, education, employment → `CSDH`
- [P141] **DEFINICION** — health inequities = socially determined health inequalities regarded as unfair/unjust; could be reduced if governments chose differently (Whitehead 1992)

## Healthcare services

- [P142] **HECHO** — universal access to PHC = important step improving population health + health equity in all countries (WHO 2008)
- [P143] **DEFINICION** — comprehensive PHC = first-level medical care + strategies for health promotion, disease prevention, community engagement, addressing local determinants

## Early child development

- [P144] **HECHO** — environments affecting early development (conception → 7 years) crucial for health prospects over life-course (Irwin et al. 2007) → `early child development`

## Income, education, and employment status

- [P145] **HECHO** — health inequities follow gradient: average health decreases at each SES step from highest → lowest (Fryers et al. 2003; Marmot & Friel 2008)
- [P146] **HECHO** — developed countries with higher inequality → worse health across ALL SES levels vs more equal societies (Wilkinson & Pickett 2009)
