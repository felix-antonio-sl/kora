# Taste checkpoints

## Naming
- Are labels precise without sounding bureaucratic?
- Do two different concepts share one muddy name?
- Does the naming fit the product's actual mental model?

## Layout and hierarchy
- Is the eye pulled to the right thing first?
- Does spacing create calm or clutter?
- Are panels and controls carrying their visual weight?

## Interaction feel
- Is there hesitation after an action?
- Is feedback timely and legible?
- Is an important control hidden behind discoverability debt?

## Workflow
- What is the one move that feels unnecessary?
- Where does confidence drop?
- Is there a missing shortcut, default, or direct path?

## Product coherence
- Does the implementation feel like the intended product, or like a demo?
- Does the surface invite serious use?
- Is there any area that feels cheap, noisy, or unfinished despite working?
