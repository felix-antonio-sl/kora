## HUMAN 3.0 — Core Architecture

Central framework for multidimensional human development. Synthesizes Spiral Dynamics, Ken Wilber's AQAL, Loevinger's ego development stages, flow psychology, and vocational theory.

| Quadrant | Domain | Scope |
|----------|--------|-------|

| Body | Personal physical world | Behavior, appearance, nutrition, training, habits, communication, grooming |
| Spirit | Collective mental world | Relationships, meaning-making, community, belonging, culture |
| Vocation | Collective physical world | Career, value creation, economic participation, legacy, systems |

## HUMAN 3.0 — Channels, Glitches, and Archetypes

### Channels

Intense, self-sustaining periods of obsessive learning or building, activated during Dissonance phase (x.1).

Indicators: time distortion, compulsive note-taking, inability to stop discussing the topic, physical excitement. Duration: 1 week to multiple years. Level 3 individuals have longer, more frequent Channels.

Process to enter a Channel:

1. Reach Dissonance phase.
2. Use distaste as fuel to push into the unknown.
3. Create a vision/aim within a specific quadrant.
4. Begin acquiring knowledge and skill.
5. Experiment until pulled into a Channel.

Channels do not automatically advance levels — require integration work.

### Glitches

High-risk tactics to force Channel entry or break through developmental plateaus.

| Glitch | Primary Quadrant | Risk |
|--------|-----------------|------|
| Psychedelics | Spirit | Psychosis at Level 1.0 |
| PEDs | Body | Permanent damage without 5+ years training foundation |
| Financial pressure | Vocation | Forces business necessity (e.g., moving into unaffordable apartment) |
| Extreme life changes | All | Relationship endings, geographic moves, career pivots |
| AI | All (Meta-Glitch) | Only glitch crossing all quadrants simultaneously |

Risk scaling by level:

- Level 1.0 + Glitch = "Death sentence" — no foundation to integrate
- Level 2.0 + Glitch = High risk/reward — requires guidance
- Level 2.5–3.0 + Glitch = Calculated risk — strong foundation allows conscious trade-offs

AI-specific risks: AI psychosis (losing touch with unaugmented reality), mind outsourcing (atrophying natural thinking), identity dissolution, capability illusion, dependency formation.

### Metatype Scoring

- Each Level 1 position = 1 point; Level 2 = 2; Level 3 = 3
- Sum across all 4 quadrants ÷ 12 = overall development score

### Cross-Quadrant Unlock Sequences

### Problem-Solving Methodology (5 Steps)

1. **Problem Recognition** — current state causes suffering; can't be ignored
2. **Problem Analysis** — which quadrant is root cause; what knowledge/skill solves it; minimum effective dose
3. **Solution Design** — daily practices (15–60 min), weekly challenges, monthly milestones
4. **Channel Pursuit** — follow excitement; allow obsessive focus; document flow-state triggers
5. **Integration & Next Problem** — consolidate gains; notice what new problem surfaces; begin cycle at higher level

### False Transformation

Imitating a higher level's behaviors without required trait development. Mechanism: developing in one domain (Mind) and assuming advancement in another (Spirit or Vocation). Produces developmental stagnation.

### Pre-Trans Fallacy (Ken Wilber)

Level 1 (pre-rational) and Level 3 (trans-rational) can both appear "non-rational" from Level 2 (rational), causing confusion about development. Occurs in both directions.

## Levels of Thinking (5D Model)

### Three Dimensions

**Lines (horizontal / width):** Domain-specific knowledge. Any domain: marketing, astrophysics, politics, religion, etc. Width = breadth of experience.

**Levels (vertical / depth):** How you think about each domain.

| Level | Name | Characterization | Tier |
|-------|------|-------------------|------|
| 0 | Instinctual | Pure survival; react to stimuli; minimal thinking | — |
| 1 | Conformist | Black/white; follow rules; obey authority; adopt others' perspectives | First |
| 2 | Individualist | Critical thinking; construct own model; own perspective | First |
| 3 | Synthesist | Own model as one among many; hold contradictions; perspectives as tools | Second |
| 4 | Generative | Create original perspectives without outside influence | Second |

First Tier (1–2) = dogmatic ("I'm right, you're wrong"). Second Tier (3–4) = reject dogma, seek truth.

**Altitude (cross-dimensional / height):** Average of all levels across domains. Higher-level traits unlock only when lower-level traits across multiple domains meet a threshold. Cannot reach Level 3 in business if required supporting domains (leadership, time management, spirituality) haven't developed.

### Four Perspectives (4D Thinking)

|  | Individual | Collective |
|--|-----------|-----------|
| **Inner (mental)** | Psychology: thoughts, emotions, beliefs | Culture: group beliefs, value systems, ideologies |
| **Outer (physical)** | Behavior, appearance, physical brain states | Systems, structures, social institutions |

Method: run any problem through all 4 perspectives before acting.

### 5th Dimension: Time (Historical Patterns)

**Master pattern — Transcend and Include (Holarchy, Arthur Koestler):** Reality is composed of holons — "whole parts," each complete in itself and a component of a larger whole. Each level transcends and includes the one before. Remove a lower level → chain collapses. Chain: atoms → molecules → cells → organs → organisms → ecosystems.

| Track | Evolution |
|-------|-----------|
| Individual physical | Atoms → Cells → Molecules → Organs → Organism |
| Individual mental | Egocentric → Ethnocentric → Worldcentric → Kosmocentric |
| Societal physical | Hoe → horse-drawn plow → machine agriculture → computers → AI |
| Societal mental | Pre-rational (conform to authority) → Rational (science/Enlightenment) → Post-rational (integrate blind spots of both) |

**What stops thinking:** Identity. Latching onto ideas/beliefs as part of who you are closes the cognitive loop. Practice: when you feel threatened by an idea, sit with it, question it, stay open.

## Identity, Consciousness, and Change

### Identity Cycle (8 Steps)

1. Want to achieve a goal
2. Perceive reality through the lens of that goal
3. Notice only information that helps achieve the goal
4. Act toward the goal and receive feedback
5. Repeat behavior until automatic and unconscious (conditioning)
6. Behavior becomes part of identity ("I am the type of person who...")
7. Defend identity to maintain psychological consistency
8. Identity shapes new goals → cycle restarts

Breaking point: interrupt between steps 6 and 7.

### 9 Stages of Ego Development (Loevinger/Greuter)

| Stage | Name | Core |
|-------|------|------|
| 1 | Impulsive | No separation between impulse and action; black-and-white |
| 2 | Self-Protective | World is dangerous; learn to protect yourself |
| 3 | Conformist | You are your group; its rules feel like reality |
| 4 | Self-Aware | Notice inner life doesn't match exterior |
| 5 | Conscientious | Build own system of principles; hold yourself accountable |
| 6 | Individualist | See principles were shaped by context; hold them loosely |
| 7 | Strategist | Work with systems while aware of own involvement |
| 8 | Construct-Aware | See all frameworks, including identity, as useful fictions |
| 9 | Unitive | Separation between self and life dissolves |

### Conceptual Survival

Animals survive physically; humans also survive psychologically. The ego preserves self-image, beliefs, values, and worldview (memes — units of culture spreading mind to mind). Threat to beliefs triggers the same fight-or-flight response as physical threats. This is why identity change is experienced as painful.

### Cybernetics Loop

From Greek kybernetikos ("good at steering"). Properties of intelligent systems:

1. Have a goal
2. Act toward that goal
3. Sense where you are
4. Compare to the goal
5. Act again based on feedback

Intelligence = capacity to iterate and persist through trial and error. Low-intelligence: get stuck, quit at roadblocks. High-intelligence: any problem solvable on a large enough timescale.

Self-discovery follows this loop: ~5–10 cycles over 3–5 years to feel on the right path.

### Three-Phase Identity Flip

[→ Three Phases in HUMAN 3.0 — Core Architecture]. Applied to identity: Discovery phase produces "6 years of progress in 6 months."

### Procrastination as Identity Threat

Procrastination occurs because survival isn't at stake. Attempting to change threatens a relevant identity. Method to override: change environment (physical and digital) to places where you feel you don't yet belong. Follow people with goals/beliefs matching your future self. When identity shifts to "person who works toward goals," discipline becomes seamless.

### Psychic Entropy

A system naturally declines into chaos unless energy is put into maintaining order. If you don't pursue growth, life only gets worse until recovery is very hard.

### Reinvention Protocol (Controlled Identity Death)

Actions:

- Move to a new physical or digital environment
- Unfollow everyone online; follow people aligned with future self
- Change what you wear, where you shop, who you see
- Drown mind in books previously avoided
- Align all information with who you want to become

Old identity dies because it is starved of reinforcing information. Resistance during this phase is a signal to lean in.

### One-Day Life Reset Protocol

**Morning — Psychological Excavation (15–30 min):**

Anti-vision diagnostic questions:

- What is the dull, persistent dissatisfaction you've learned to live with?
- What do you complain about repeatedly but never change? (List 3.) For each: what would an observer conclude you actually want?
- What truth would be unbearable to admit to someone you respect?
- Describe an average Tuesday in 5/10/end-of-life years if nothing changes
- Who is already living that future version of you?
- What identity would you have to give up to change?
- If current behavior is self-protection, what are you protecting, and what is that costing?

Minimum Viable Vision construction:

- If you could snap to a different life in 3 years, what would an average Tuesday look like?
- What would you have to believe about yourself for that life to feel natural?
- One thing you would do this week if you were already that person?

**Throughout the Day — Interrupting Autopilot:**

Scheduled reminders with embedded questions:

- 11:00am: What am I avoiding right now?
- 1:30pm: If someone filmed the last 2 hours, what would they conclude I want?
- 3:15pm: Am I moving toward the life I hate or want?
- 5:00pm: What's the most important thing I'm pretending isn't important?
- 7:30pm: What did I do today out of identity protection rather than genuine desire?
- 9:00pm: When did I feel most alive/dead today?

Walking contemplation prompts:

- What would change if I stopped needing people to see me as [identity]?
- Where am I trading aliveness for safety?
- What's the smallest version of the person I want to become that I could be tomorrow?

**Evening — Synthesis:**

- After today, what feels most true about why you've been stuck?
- What is the actual enemy? (Internal pattern/belief, not circumstances.)
- One sentence: what you refuse to let your life become (compressed anti-vision)
- One sentence: what you're building toward (Vision MVP)
- Goals as lenses: one-year lens (what confirms the old pattern is broken), one-month lens (what keeps the one-year lens possible), daily lens (2–3 actions the future self would do)

## Agency, Purpose, and Meaning

### Agency

"The tendency to initiate action towards a goal without outside prompting, instruction, or permission" — plus an undying commitment to iteration. Agency = action + iteration.

### 5 Properties of High-Agency People

1. **Iterate without permission** — act toward a goal; when it doesn't work, reflect, adjust, try again
2. **Treat life as one giant experiment** — idea → own goal → hypothesis → test/tinker/research → fail → narrow down → find what works
3. **Believe in the difficult** — three goal types: Easy (doable now), Difficult (doable with skill/resource acquisition), Impossible (requires completing a series of difficult goals first). Reference: Seligman's learned helplessness experiment — dogs trained to endure unavoidable shocks wouldn't escape even when escape was easy.
4. **Are context creators, not content creators** — content without context (vision, personality, throughline, brand mission) is meaningless. AI can generate content but lacks context.
5. **Are generalists** — Shakespeare required deep knowledge of human nature, language, classical literature, stagecraft, religion, philosophy, military tactics, music, navigation. "A jack of all trades is a master of none" is a misquote; full: "but oftentimes better than a master of one."

### 5 Human Capabilities (Irreplaceable by AGI)

| Capability | Definition |
|-----------|-----------|
| Computation (mental) | No limit on what can be computed given enough time and memory |
| Transformation (physical) | Creation; physical construction still requires time regardless of compute speed |
| Variation | Infinite idea generation; agency enables exploration of any position in idea space |
| Selection | Error-correction; cumulative progress via learning from mistakes |
| Attention | Ability to change perspective/focus; put on different lenses when problems arise |

### Liberating Arts (Devon Eriksen, adapted)

Seven skill-capacities for self-reliant people:

1. **Logic** — deriving truth from known facts
2. **Statistics** — understanding implications of data
3. **Rhetoric** — persuading and spotting persuasion
4. **Research** — gathering information on unknown subjects
5. **Psychology** — discerning true motives of self and others
6. **Investment** — managing and growing assets
7. **Agency** — deciding what to pursue and acting without permission

Three demands that force development: (1) build your own thing and put it in front of people, (2) write publicly and consistently, (3) use AI to do previously impossible things, not just to avoid work.

### Deep Generalism

Pre-printing press: knowledge scarce; specialization forced. Post-Gutenberg: 20 million books in 50 years. Internet = second printing press; AI accelerates further. First Renaissance enabled generalists (Da Vinci, Michelangelo). Current moment enables the same.

Specialist = tool; tools get replaced. Generalist = adapts to any niche through knowledge and tool-building. The ultimate moat in an AI world: a unique opinion only your specific life experience could generate.

## One-Person Business Model

### Two Business Paths

| | Skill-Based | Development-Based (recommended) |
|--|------------|-------------------------------|
| Method | Learn marketable skill → teach it → sell product/service | Pursue own goals (brand) → teach what you learn (content) → help others achieve faster (product) |
| Limitation | One-dimensional; builds into a second 9-to-5 | — |
| Advantage | Fast initial clarity | Multi-interest compatible; identity and niche evolve together |
| Validation | External market research | Automatic — you've lived the transformation |

The 4 eternal markets: health, wealth, relationships, happiness. Turn yourself into the customer avatar.

### Brand, Content, Offer (3 Pillars)

1. **Brand** — who you are, what you help people achieve, why people should care. Not a profile picture or bio. Brand = accumulation of ideas in reader's mind after 3–6 months.
2. **Content** — ideas, opinions, teachings that attract people to the brand. Selection criteria: intersection of Performance (will others care?) and Excitement (do you care?).
3. **Offer** — product, service, and landing page.

### "You Are the Niche"

"I help ___ do ___ without ___" = outdated. Niche is illustrated by your body of work. Identity evolves as you do; niche evolves with you. Solve your own problems → sell the solution.

### Mission-Based vs. Topic-Based Niching

| | Topic-Based | Mission-Based |
|--|------------|--------------|
| Definition | Choose target audience + topic | Lead with a transformation you personally pursue |
| Content constraint | Narrow | Any aligned idea qualifies |
| Identity risk | High (pivot = audience loss) | Low (mission evolves with you) |
| Best for | Rapid monetization clarity; topic experts | Multi-passionate creators; long-term builders |

Mission-based 4-step process:

1. **Define enemy** — what way of life is the bane of your existence
2. **Create identity for people to adopt** — name the mission so it implies the archetype (e.g., "Modern Mastery")
3. **Build a world to explore** — enough content for someone to binge in a week. Frame: small-scale MCU — long-form (movies), short-form (spin-off shows), products (action figures)
4. **80% why, 20% how** — people scroll social media passively; persuade why before teaching how

### Digital Asset Ladder

1. Prioritize media — content on platform matching your medium
2. Start a service business — freelancing, coaching, consulting; fewer clients at higher prices
3. Productize your service — systematize or build an information product
4. Continue pursuing uncertainty — software, physical products, investments

Digital asset categories: Media (content + audience), Data (audience/customer base), Code (software).

Knowledge that can't be trained for; comes from pursuing genuine curiosity; feels like play; often at the edge of knowledge. Years of obsessive, fine-tuned experience and taste = moat. AI makes average output free; the moat is uncopyable judgment.

## Content, Communication, and Persuasion

### Writing as Meta-Skill

Writing = the meta-skill because mastery requires writing in public, which forces: error-correction, feedback loops, inadvertent learning of psychology/marketing/sales/persuasion/human nature. Writing is permissionless leverage; foundation of all media (posts, scripts, newsletters, outlines). Learning sequence: writing → thinking → earning; all three improve together.

### Inner Album of Greatest Hits

A pool of 8–10 of your biggest ideas, already thought through hundreds of times, connectable to almost any topic. Best speakers don't answer the host's question directly — they state their best idea on the topic, then expand. Repeating pre-validated ideas is compounding, not stagnation.

### Three Articulation Frameworks

**Beginner — PAS (Problem-Amplify-Solution):**

- Problem: state a relatable problem
- Amplify: illustrate negative outcome if unsolved
- Solution: one sentence or short list (short form); full key points (long form)
- Problem + Amplification = the hook

**Intermediate — Pyramid Principle:**

1. Main idea first (answer-first)
2. Key arguments supporting it (3–5 points)
3. Detailed evidence (data, examples, analysis)

**Advanced — Cross-Domain Synthesis:**

1. Problem and amplify (introduction)
2. Pull a pattern/concept from an unrelated discipline (e.g., entropy from physics to explain distraction)
3. Unique process or solution from your own contemplation

### Content Legos (Building Blocks)

When stuck within a section, cycle through: pain point, example, personal story, statistic, metaphor (explain as if to a child), quote, reframe (alternative perspective), What/How/Why question.

### Content Ecosystem / Waterfall

1. One newsletter/week (anchored to content pillars)
2. Newsletter → soft script for one YouTube video/week → posted to podcast platforms
3. Short-form posts pulled from newsletter ideas or original
4. Best posts become Reels/Shorts scripts (already validated)
5. Other posts become carousels for LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube Community

Rule: fewer, higher-quality pieces. One long-form/week + a few short-form/day → distribute across all platforms.

### Idea Museum

Ruthless curation of notes, ideas, and inspiration sources ("swipe file").

- Gauge performance: check engagement of posts from other accounts
- Gauge excitement: notice when you feel you're wasting something valuable if you don't write it down

3–5 high-signal sources: (1) old or little-known books, (2) curated blogs/accounts, (3) 5 social accounts that consistently post great ideas.

### Content Writing — 80/20

1. Write down ideas constantly
2. Immediately identify the pain point or benefit
3. Save ideas with structures/frameworks you like (imitate structure, use your own topic)
4. Practice until second nature

Content quality formula: Good idea + pain point it solves or benefit it promises + well-illustrated reason to care = effective content.

Biggest mistakes: (1) not illustrating why an idea matters, (2) not understanding how content connects to income (leads to algorithm-chasing).

### AI-Assisted Content Analysis Prompt

> "Do a comprehensive analysis on this social post. The overall idea, how the sentences are structured, and choice of words. Analyze why people engage with it, why it works so well, what psychological tactics are being used, and how I can replicate this style step-by-step with my own ideas."

For deconstructing any content format into a replicable structural template.

### AI-Assisted Writing Workflow

1. Write extensive outline of points and ideas (informal)
2. Feed own books and past content into context
3. Flesh out ideas while AI researches alongside
4. Comb through drafts: cut, redirect dead sections, push on live ones
5. Add commentary on weak spots to think deeper

Author retains thinking/judgment; AI handles research labor.

### Advertising as Universal Meta-Skill

If you don't advertise your work → no income → forced to sell someone else's → lose creative control. The "starving artist" is a modern myth; historical artists (Michelangelo, Shakespeare) were effective marketers.

Unconscious advertising everyone does: describing a weekend, persuading friends on dinner, job interviews, recommending shows, choosing outfits, storytelling. The choice: do it unconsciously (manipulative by default) vs. consciously (ethical and effective).

### Protégé Effect

Teaching accelerates the teacher's learning more than the student's. Writing publicly about what you're learning is the highest-leverage practice method.

2. Breakthrough Advertising (Eugene Schwartz) — customer psychology and offer positioning
3. Flow (Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi) — flow psychology applied to value creation

## Learning, Mastery, and Focus

### Project-Based Learning

Core principle: the best way to learn is to build a real-world project and only search for information when you need it. Learning speed is directly correlated with project progress, not tutorial consumption. True learning threatens current identity; false learning (tutorials, school) reinforces it.

How to start:

1. Choose something to build that moves the needle toward your vision
2. Brain-dump everything
3. Save 3–5 sources of inspiration to emulate
4. Study those sources; break down their structure
5. Outline into sections, milestones, and knowledge gaps
6. Start building — only search when stuck

Rule: 90%+ of information is not retained without a real-world project acting as a filter. The project must be a stepping stone toward your ideal future, not just a practice exercise.

### The Mastery Method (7 Steps)

**Step 1 — Expand Your Mind (Vision Generation):**
Set a massive goal in one of 4 dimensions: mind, body, spirit, vocation. Goal is for direction and filtration. Questions: ideal day with unlimited money, ideal environment, ideal family, ideal work, how you want to look/feel. Map ideal day hour by hour. Supplement with anti-vision (opposite of every answer). Filter every opportunity through vision.

**Step 2 — Hierarchy of Goals:**

| Scope | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| 10-year goals | Vision and opportunity filtration |
| 1-year goals | Direction |
| Monthly goals | Milestones |
| Weekly goals | Cadence |
| Daily: 3–5 priority tasks | Execute first thing before distractions |

**Step 3 — How to Learn:**
Turn goals into projects. Ideate → milestones → outline → brain-dump → build and learn in unison. Each problem solved = "a small evolution" that was unsolvable from previous consciousness level.

**Step 4 — Skills Are Groups of Techniques:**
You don't "learn a skill" — you accumulate techniques. Each technique = a tool that reduces dependence on current survival strategies. Example: copywriting decomposes into capturing attention, holding attention, enhancing perceived value, inspiring action. Dozens of techniques per sub-skill. Meta-principle: "Everything is a skill. Life is a skill that encapsulates health, wealth, and relationships."

**Step 5 — Reinvent Yourself:**
[→ Reinvention Protocol in Identity section]

**Step 6 — Nature's Compass (Mistakes as Feedback):**
Mistakes on a self-directed path = directional signals. Mistakes on a conventional path = just failure. Method: force highest-version perspective → let it unveil opportunities → act → use mistakes as direction for next choice.

**Step 7 — Self-Experimentation:**
Treat all projects as science projects. Hypothesize → experiment with techniques → document → double down on results. Warning: attaching to one method (e.g., veganism) misidentifies modality as cause. Solution: test multiple modalities → extract underlying principles → build personalized system.

### Mental Frame

Anti-Vision → Vision → Standards → Projects → Daily Levers → feedback loop.

Standards are mostly unconscious; absorbed from environment. Change environments to raise them. Anti-standards (constraints): "I will not work more than 4 hours/day" forces discovery of higher-leverage opportunities. Creativity is born within constraints.

| Vision | How you win | Win condition |

### Three Ingredients of Individual Success

1. **Self-education** — direct your own learning
2. **Self-interest** — follow genuine interest (not institutional); produces intrinsic motivation
3. **Self-sufficiency** — refuse to outsource judgment, learning, agency

Mutually reinforcing: self-interest motivates self-education; self-education enables self-sufficiency; self-sufficiency clarifies self-interest.

### Three Determinants of Focus

**1. Clarity + Importance + Urgency:**
- Clarity: task challenging enough to be novel; if overwhelming, break into sub-goals
- Importance: fully understand where life ends up without goal AND what potential exists with it
- Urgency: what compels you to sit down right now

**2. Intrinsic Motivator Stack (5 stacking states):**

|-------|---------------|-------------------|

| Passion | More dopamine + norepinephrine | Sufficient investment |
| Purpose | Dopamine + serotonin + oxytocin | Attach to something greater |

**3. Hunter-Gatherer Time Structure:**

Between work blocks: 30-minute walk; audiobook or podcast. Most lever-moving tasks demand creativity. Example: entire business runs on 2 hours of writing/day.

Quantitative claim: "If you focus on the small quantity of lever-moving tasks, you can do 8 hours of work in 4 hours or less."

### Dopamine Detox Protocol (30 Days)

Overstimulation raises the dopamine threshold → everything below phone/gambling/porn threshold is labeled boring → destroys capacity for flow states.

Three mechanisms: (1) boredom activates breakthrough insights and reveals authentic desires, (2) hedonic treadmill reversal — deprivation makes simple pleasures enjoyable ("beginner's mind"), (3) creates space for clarity by closing Zeigarnik loops (brain holds onto incomplete tasks, draining cognitive resources).

**7-Step Protocol:**

1. **Pain & Gain Story** — create two visceral, specific stories: where life ends up in pain vs. who you become if you change. Establishes identity shift precondition.

2. **Monk Mode (30 days)** — eliminate: binge tech (social media, streaming, gaming, news), unnatural sexual stimulation, consumables (alcohol, drugs, junk food), thrill-seeking (gambling, shopping sprees).

3. **Walk 10,000 Steps/Day** — actual sweet spot: 7,000–8,000 steps. Schedule: 20 min after breakfast, lunch, dinner. Benefits: low-intensity cardio, improved digestion/blood pressure/insulin sensitivity, circadian health from morning/afternoon/evening light, visible leanness improvement within 7+ days.

4. **Resistance Training** — 30+ min exercise. Reliable flow trigger. Creates BDNF (neuroplasticity). Boosts mitochondrial density. More muscle = higher BMR.

5. **Elimination Diet** — recommended baseline: Vertical Diet (Stan Efferding). Simple, nutrient-dense, reveals hidden food sensitivities. 30+ days rewires eating habits.

6. **Build a New Project** — [→ Project-Based Learning method above]

7. **Reflect 10 Minutes Every Night** — 3-2-1 Framework: 3 wins, 2 lessons, 1 intention for morning. Physical notebook only. Closes Zeigarnik loops; builds 30-day feedback dataset.

Recommended frequency: at least once per year. Conscious attention processes ~50 bits of information per second.

## AI and the Future of Work

### Three Power Transfer Layers

| Social media | Employers/publishers/media (audience leverage) | Persuasion |

### Skills Abstraction Pattern

Each technology wave pushes humans up one layer of judgment:

| Old role | New role | Technology |
|----------|----------|-----------|
| Scribe | Editor | Printing press |
| Hand-weaver | Machine operator | Industrial Revolution |
| Typesetter | Designer | Desktop publishing |

Printing press: 3,600 pages/workday vs. hand-copying. Steam press (1814): 1,100 pages/hour vs. 250. Mechanized cotton spinning: 500x output/worker. AlphaFold (2020): protein folding in minutes vs. months of PhD lab work.

### Three Archetypes of Response to Disruption

| Archetype | Behavior |
|-----------|----------|
| Resisters | Attach identity to old methods; see tools as threats |
| Waiters | See change but assume it'll blow over; depend on external structure |
| The Curious | Experiment without romanticizing past or fearing future; understand lag before tools become useful |

### Taste = New Intelligence

Art is defined by vision, soul, and final product — not the tool or hours invested. "A word is the new brush stroke." AI is a programming language for language itself. Taste separates art from slop. "If you can't create art with AI, you were never an artist to begin with."

When supply of competent output is infinite, curation/discernment is the only scarce good. Infinite library problem: a library with every possible book is useless without curation.

### Silicon vs. Carbon Division of Labor

| Domain | Handler |
|--------|---------|
| Speed, repetition, necessity | Silicon (machines) |
| Story, novelty, myth, meaning | Carbon (humans) |

"Silicon solves utility so carbon can transcend to meaning."

### The Swap Test (Koe)

"If you could swap the creator and the creation would be just as valuable, then AI can replace it. If the creation only works because you made it, then that's your edge."

Work with an "objective floor" (documentation, summaries, instructions) → hand to AI. Work dependent on the creator's evolving perspective → cannot be swapped.

### 5 Things AI Cannot Access

1. **Cannot think *from* your perspective** — only *about* or *in the style of* it
2. **Cannot replicate your energy signature** — cannot select what to focus on and why it matters *now*
3. **Cannot perform genuine sensemaking** — cannot decide what information means or how to frame it
4. **Has no trajectory** — no stakes of death, no natural time compression
5. **Has no evolving taste** — does not grow; does not disagree with its past output

"AI can copy anything, but it cannot copy what happens next until it has already happened."

### Post-Labor Economics (David Shapiro framework)

Economic cycle at risk: companies pay workers → workers spend → companies earn. AI disrupts if it crosses the "better, faster, cheaper, safer" threshold.

Three household income mechanisms: (1) Wages, (2) Transfers (UBI/government), (3) Capital Income (assets). Solution direction: broadening capital participation. Transfers alone cause political instability and lose price signals.

### Jobs Persisting Despite Automation Superiority

1. High-liability roles (someone to legally blame)
2. Statutory positions (legally required humans)
3. Experience economy (bartenders, boutiques, art)
4. Meaning makers (navigating the human experience)
5. Relationship/trust jobs (sales, diplomacy, negotiation)

### How to Prepare (3 Prescriptions)

1. **Become a philosopher-builder** — merge polar ends: thinker + builder, programmer + marketer, spiritual + practical. "Specialized generalist" is the peak. Job titles are merging.
2. **Become a filter for ideas** — curation is the new IQ test. Requires: defined values, worldview, vision. Forge your own philosophy through trial and error.
3. **Become an AI orchestrator** — key decision: when to use AI vs. do by hand (determined by what you enjoy). Specialize in craft; orchestrate AI for everything else. Best learning method: "try automating yourself out of a job."

## Mitochondrial Biohacking Stack

Performance-focused supplement protocol targeting mitochondrial function rather than nootropics. Stacking keeps individual doses in safer ranges through synergistic effects.

### Methylene Blue (oral capsule)

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Dose | 3–15 mg/day (author: 10 mg, sometimes 20 mg) |

| Risks | Serotonin syndrome (do NOT combine with SSRIs or 5-HTP); nitric oxide inhibition at high doses; pro-oxidant at high doses; contraindicated in G6PD deficiency |
