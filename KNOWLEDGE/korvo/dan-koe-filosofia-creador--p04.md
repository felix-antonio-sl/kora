# Creator Philosophy: Dan Koe — Synthesized Corpus - Parte 04

## AI and the Future of Work

### Three Power Transfer Layers

| Layer | Power transferred from | Individual superpower unlocked |
|-------|----------------------|-------------------------------|
| Internet | Schools/institutions (knowledge access) | Learning |
| Social media | Employers/publishers/media (audience leverage) | Persuasion |
| AI | Traditional gatekeepers/intermediaries | Execution (automation, creation, delegation) |

### Skills Abstraction Pattern

Each technology wave pushes humans up one layer of judgment:

| Old role | New role | Technology |
|----------|----------|-----------|
| Scribe | Editor | Printing press |
| Hand-weaver | Machine operator | Industrial Revolution |
| Typesetter | Designer | Desktop publishing |
| Content creator | Context creator | AI |

Printing press: 3,600 pages/workday vs. hand-copying. Steam press (1814): 1,100 pages/hour vs. 250. Mechanized cotton spinning: 500x output/worker. AlphaFold (2020): protein folding in minutes vs. months of PhD lab work.

### Three Archetypes of Response to Disruption

| Archetype | Behavior |
|-----------|----------|
| Resisters | Attach identity to old methods; see tools as threats |
| Waiters | See change but assume it'll blow over; depend on external structure |
| The Curious | Experiment without romanticizing past or fearing future; understand lag before tools become useful |

### Taste = New Intelligence

Art is defined by vision, soul, and final product — not the tool or hours invested. "A word is the new brush stroke." AI is a programming language for language itself. Taste separates art from slop. "If you can't create art with AI, you were never an artist to begin with."

When supply of competent output is infinite, curation/discernment is the only scarce good. Infinite library problem: a library with every possible book is useless without curation.

### Silicon vs. Carbon Division of Labor

| Domain | Handler |
|--------|---------|
| Speed, repetition, necessity | Silicon (machines) |
| Story, novelty, myth, meaning | Carbon (humans) |

"Silicon solves utility so carbon can transcend to meaning."

### The Swap Test (Koe)

"If you could swap the creator and the creation would be just as valuable, then AI can replace it. If the creation only works because you made it, then that's your edge."

Work with an "objective floor" (documentation, summaries, instructions) → hand to AI. Work dependent on the creator's evolving perspective → cannot be swapped.

### 5 Things AI Cannot Access

1. **Cannot think *from* your perspective** — only *about* or *in the style of* it
2. **Cannot replicate your energy signature** — cannot select what to focus on and why it matters *now*
3. **Cannot perform genuine sensemaking** — cannot decide what information means or how to frame it
4. **Has no trajectory** — no stakes of death, no natural time compression
5. **Has no evolving taste** — does not grow; does not disagree with its past output

"AI can copy anything, but it cannot copy what happens next until it has already happened."

### Post-Labor Skill Stack (Hierarchy)

| Rank | Skill | Role |
|------|-------|------|
| 1 | Agency | Meta-skill; creates unique story; money shifts from productivity metric → tool to express agency |
| 2 | Taste | Discernment; curation over creation; requires building something of your own |
| 3 | Perspective | Increasing human capacity; developmental psychology; expand by allowing disorientation |
| 4 | Persuasion | Get people to care; attention mechanisms; art merges with business |
| 5 | Technical Know-How | Utilizing AI and tools; lowest priority; learned automatically by building |

### Post-Labor Economics (David Shapiro framework)

Economic cycle at risk: companies pay workers → workers spend → companies earn. AI disrupts if it crosses the "better, faster, cheaper, safer" threshold.

Three household income mechanisms: (1) Wages, (2) Transfers (UBI/government), (3) Capital Income (assets). Solution direction: broadening capital participation. Transfers alone cause political instability and lose price signals.

### Jobs Persisting Despite Automation Superiority

1. High-liability roles (someone to legally blame)
2. Statutory positions (legally required humans)
3. Experience economy (bartenders, boutiques, art)
4. Meaning makers (navigating the human experience)
5. Relationship/trust jobs (sales, diplomacy, negotiation)

### How to Prepare (3 Prescriptions)

1. **Become a philosopher-builder** — merge polar ends: thinker + builder, programmer + marketer, spiritual + practical. "Specialized generalist" is the peak. Job titles are merging.
2. **Become a filter for ideas** — curation is the new IQ test. Requires: defined values, worldview, vision. Forge your own philosophy through trial and error.
3. **Become an AI orchestrator** — key decision: when to use AI vs. do by hand (determined by what you enjoy). Specialize in craft; orchestrate AI for everything else. Best learning method: "try automating yourself out of a job."

### AI Window

"The people who figure this out in the next 12–36 months will be seen as a different species." The gap compounds monthly. Sam Altman: 10-person $1B companies are near-term; betting pool exists for first 1-person $1B company.

## Mitochondrial Biohacking Stack

Performance-focused supplement protocol targeting mitochondrial function rather than nootropics. Stacking keeps individual doses in safer ranges through synergistic effects.

### Methylene Blue (oral capsule)

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Dose | 3–15 mg/day (author: 10 mg, sometimes 20 mg) |
| Mechanism | Enhances electron transport chain; increases ATP; reduces oxidative stress; MAOI activity (prevents breakdown of serotonin, dopamine, norepinephrine) |
| Benefits | Cognition, neuroprotection, anti-aging, improved O2 capacity in red blood cells |
| Risks | Serotonin syndrome (do NOT combine with SSRIs or 5-HTP); nitric oxide inhibition at high doses; pro-oxidant at high doses; contraindicated in G6PD deficiency |

### SLU-PP-332 (oral capsule)

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Dose | 200–600 mcg/day; split due to 1–2 hour half-life (author: 250 mcg morning/pre-workout/afternoon) |
| Mechanism | ERR alpha/beta/gamma agonist; exercise mimetic; activates aerobic exercise gene pathways; mitochondrial biogenesis |
| Benefits | Fat loss, improved insulin sensitivity, enhanced endurance from first dose, organ health/longevity |
| Risks | Increased body temperature/sweating; no long-term human studies; potential liver enzyme elevation (mitigate with NAC/Tudca/milk thistle) |
| Schedule | Training days only (5x/week) |

### MOTS-c (subcutaneous injection)

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Dose | Start 0.25 mg tolerance test → 0.5–1 mg/day (author: 1 mg, 5–7x/week) |
| Mechanism | 16 amino acid polypeptide produced by mitochondria; released in response to metabolic/oxidative stress; regulates glucose and fatty acid metabolism |
| Benefits | ATP synthesis, pregnenolone precursor, exercise mimetic (animal studies: comparable cardiac/metabolic benefits to treadmill), longevity (animal study: +4.6–7% lifespan increase) |
| Risks | Injection site rash (do not exceed 2.5 mg subcutaneous); anaphylactic shock risk above 5 mg; insomnia at high late doses; cardiac hypertrophy risk combined with anabolic steroids |

### Retatrutide (subcutaneous injection, weekly)

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Type | Triple agonist: GLP-1 + GIP + Glucagon receptors |
| Dose | Clinical trials: 0.5–12 mg/week; anecdotal sweet spot: 3–6 mg; start at 1–2 mg/week split into 3 doses (MWF), titrate every 1–2 weeks |
| Benefits | Up to 25–30% body weight reduction at 12 mg over 48 weeks (clinical trial); reduces food noise/cravings; potential dopamine modulation for addictive behaviors; improves lipids, blood pressure, insulin sensitivity |
| Risks | GI effects (nausea, vomiting, diarrhea); heart rate increase 6–12 BPM; rare acute kidney injury/pancreatitis; rebound weight regain on cessation (mitigate with high-protein diet + new eating habits) |
| Note | Preferred over semaglutide due to less nausea |
